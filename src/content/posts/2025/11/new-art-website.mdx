I have some great news!

Currently I now have my art portfolio up and ready to go!

Right now, I used Svelte with my art website. While I really loved how simple it was to worth with compared to React, I feel it's missing some features that I need that React had

I would love to use Svelte for really simple websites, and despite me having a few gripes with it, I want to keep it in my web dev toolbelt.

I'm thinking of using Vue.js for my next portfolio website, which would be my game dev portfolio.
