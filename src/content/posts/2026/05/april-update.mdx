## Trying out GameMaker
I don't know how I didn't consider GameMaker for making games (it's even in the name). Especially seeing how many great indie games have been made in them, how popular of a choice it is for 2D indie games, and how easy it is to put things together.

I really wanted a game engine that could replace GDevelop in terms of fast prototyping. As much as I love Defold, I also like having a game engine that can easily piece things together, or test for future prototyping. GameMaker seems to help with this a lot, and has a longer reputation than an engine like Defold. I still want to use Defold as an option in case I can't use GameMaker, or if I want to focus on more mobile games and tighter performance.

As much as I want to keep using GDevelop, I just can't continue with it due to how much they keep pushing and selling their AI over other features. It can get especially frustrating when the engine could benefit towards non-programmers like artists, narrative designers, and other creatives but a lot of them would understandably don't want to use a game engine that would have AI integrated with their work.

That being said, I'm surprised with how simple GameMaker is with prototyping and making a game. Granted, you don't do block coding like GDevelop, but the drag and drop + GML scripting in GameMaker feels so easy to put together.

I'm considering GameMaker as a potential engine to work with for my Midnight Express project, mostly due to the ease of putting things together, the active community and resources going on for years, and how easy it is to export to many platforms (including consoles if I consider it in the future).

I still need to experiment to see if it's best for me and my game project. I'm hoping at the very least, I can have another engine in my toolbelt!
