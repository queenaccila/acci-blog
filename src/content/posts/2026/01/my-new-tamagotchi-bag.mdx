I usually don't buy a lot of merch, but I went to Anime Los Angeles this year on a whim and I don't regret it at all. I didn't get a chance to go last year because I got sick, but I'm really happy I ended up going!

Going to the convention, I made a goal to find mostly Tamagotchi merch. I don't know why but I was just craving to get Tamagotchi merch since I had a huge lack of it other than my Paradise. Little did I know a ton of booths were selling Tamagotchi merch, and it was really tempting to buy so much stuff (luckily I managed to get up to my budget and not overspend).

Usually when I go to ALA I always make one giant purchase as a self-indulgent spending for myself. This year, I bought a really cute Tamagotchi tote bag! Not only that, but I also got a set of acrylic coasters! (Totebag artist is [BabylonHolic](https://babylonholic.bigcartel.com/]))

I hope to go again next year! I always love seeing the cool things artists are making!
